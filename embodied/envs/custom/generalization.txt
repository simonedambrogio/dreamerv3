**Environment**
*Description*
	This task is designed to test generalisation. There are 4 main states, which I refer to as trial, which are repeated over and over again.
	Image1, image2, and image3 are abstract containers of types of images. 
	Different stimulus mapping to image1-3 is referred as sub-task.
	Every 100 trials, there is a new sub-task: three new random stimulus are assigned to image1-3. 
	E.g. if in the current task image1 is a dragon, in task_{t+1} image1 is another random stimulus,, e.g. a flower.
	In the description below, the * indicates any of the state (with uniform probability) or action.

*Actions* 
	1. left
	2. right
	3. wait
